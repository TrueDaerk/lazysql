# lazysql wiki

An [OKF 0.2](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md)
knowledge bundle: every file below `wiki/` except this index and `log.md` is a
concept with YAML frontmatter. Concept IDs are bundle-relative paths without
`.md`.

## design

- [design/tui-shell-architecture](design/tui-shell-architecture.md) — Design Decision — root model, panel structs, update routing order, modal closing rule.
- [design/keybindings-single-source](design/keybindings-single-source.md) — Design Decision — one `key.Binding` table behind dispatch, options bar, actions menu and `?`.
- [design/db-driver-abstraction](design/db-driver-abstraction.md) — Design Decision — one generic Driver over `database/sql` plus a Dialect per engine; UI never imports concrete SQL drivers.
- [design/connection-secrets](design/connection-secrets.md) — Design Decision — connections in TOML, passwords only in the OS keyring or a per-connect prompt.
- [design/connection-form-modal](design/connection-form-modal.md) — Design Decision — one reusable multi-field modal with dynamic visibility, section headers and a cursor-following scroll window; `ctrl+t` dials the shown profile without saving.
- [design/connection-form-ux](design/connection-form-ux.md) — Design Decision — issue #144 engine-first two-step connection flow: digit-shortcut engine picker, engine-specific sectioned forms, draft-preserving transitions, keystroke economics, and the rejected alternatives (including the superseded #129 flat list).
- [design/path-completion-in-forms](design/path-completion-in-forms.md) — Design Decision — the pure `internal/pathcomplete` engine plus a `pathSuggest` holder on `formModal`; `tab` completes the SQLite/DuckDB File field while candidates exist (extending to the shared prefix, then cycling candidates once nothing more can be extended), `↓`/`↑` walk the open list, `enter` accepts the highlighted candidate instead of submitting, `esc` dismisses the list before cancelling the form, and otherwise every key keeps its normal form meaning, with `~`/env-var expansion, directory/extension-ranked candidates, and the candidate list rendered as a bordered lipgloss layer floating under the field — anchored from the field's offset inside the rendered box, clamped/flipped/shrunk to the terminal — so the form's own height never changes with it.
- [design/catalog-browsing](design/catalog-browsing.md) — Design Decision — async catalog loads, stale-reply rule, inline fuzzy filter, pseudo-database for single-namespace engines, pinned-database scoping (the [2]/[3] panel split it describes was superseded by the object tree).
- [design/object-tree-panel](design/object-tree-panel.md) — Design Decision — `[2] Databases` and `[3] Tables` merged into one expandable `[2] Objects` tree (database → category → object), flattened into the existing list panel, categories loaded lazily and cached until `R`, Triggers as a first-class category with a read-only definition view, and a fuzzy filter that narrows the expanded level while keeping matching branches' ancestors.
- [design/data-grid](design/data-grid.md) — Design Decision — one-page-at-a-time main view, derived scroll windows, the `panelMain` focus target, the typed row filter with parse-first parameter binding, and the per-row `│`/`─`/`┼` table borders.
- [design/inline-where-filter](design/inline-where-filter.md) — Design Decision — `/` types a `WHERE` clause into a line inside the grid instead of opening a popup, the immutable `SELECT * FROM <relation> WHERE ` prefix rendered as the textinput's prompt from `db.FilterPrefixSQL`, the routing step that makes the line swallow the global keys, and the per-connection + per-relation filter history keyed by the same `Entry.Connection`/`Database`/`Table` fields the query history is filtered by (`history.InRelation`), in its own JSON Lines file next to the statement history.
- [design/grid-cursor-window](design/grid-cursor-window.md) — Design Decision — the grid's scroll window as a clamped stored offset instead of a value derived from the cursor, one `gridLayout` behind the render, the click hit test and `clampCursor`, budgeting the horizontal-scroll hint line, and clamping the cursor off the phantom rows when the changeset is discarded or committed.
- [design/cell-detail-popup](design/cell-detail-popup.md) — Design Decision — `v`'s content-based JSON/hex-dump/plain-text classification, the JSON cell's collapsible tree (order-preserving token parse, fold state flattened lazily, `j`/`k`/`enter`/`l`/`h`), the title's type + byte length, and why the modal's `y` copies the raw value, never the rendering.
- [design/row-detail-view](design/row-detail-view.md) — Design Decision — `x`'s two-column name/type/value list for the cursor row, reusing the grid's staged-edit/staged-insert/staged-delete detection and the cell detail popup's `v`, with no modal stack of its own.
- [design/foreign-key-navigation](design/foreign-key-navigation.md) — Design Decision — `g`/`G`/`ctrl+o` foreign-key navigation, `db.FKFilter` building the parameterized jump filter, the `dataView`-wholesale jump history, the per-namespace reverse scan, and why a NULL key refuses instead of filtering.
- [design/main-view-tabs](design/main-view-tabs.md) — Design Decision — Data/Structure/Indexes/DDL/Relations tabs behind one metadata fetch, `<`/`>` cycling instead of digits, and what survives a relation change.
- [design/relations-tab](design/relations-tab.md) — Design Decision — the per-table foreign-key hub view (outgoing above, incoming below), why it is not an ERD, how the incoming half reuses the namespace scan and its cache, and why `enter` walks unfiltered.
- [design/schema-diff](design/schema-diff.md) — Design Decision — the normalized `db.Schema` snapshot over the existing introspection calls, family-scoped type-synonym normalization, fresh dials for both sides, and why the report generates no migration SQL.
- [design/staged-changeset](design/staged-changeset.md) — Design Decision — cell edits, row deletes and row inserts stage into one engine-agnostic changeset, rows are identified only by their primary key, and the commit runs every statement in one transaction.
- [design/copy-and-export](design/copy-and-export.md) — Design Decision — one incremental serializer package behind both the `y` copy menu and the `E` file export (CSV/JSON/SQL/Markdown), why a clipboard copy is capped while a file export streams, how NULL is spelled per format, why a query result exports by re-running (`Driver.QueryStream`) rather than re-paging and only offers SQL/INSERT when `db.SingleTableSelect` can name the one table, and how the export reports progress and cancels.
- [design/grid-multi-row-selection](design/grid-multi-row-selection.md) — Design Decision — the grid's `gridSelection` (row anchor + cell cursor, plus an opt-in column span behind `shift+←`/`shift+→`) behind `ctrl+v` or `shift+up`/`shift+down`, why it is dropped on every sort/filter/page/reload instead of remapped, why `ctrl+v` is free while `ctrl+c` is bound only while a selection is up (ahead of `Quit`, behind `CancelQuery`), how the selection scopes extend `copyMenu` rather than duplicate it, and how `e` stages one pending change per selected row through the one edit modal (`bulkTargets` resolving each row to its primary key at open time).
- [design/ddl-export](design/ddl-export.md) — Design Decision — `E` on the DDL tab writes `TableDDL` verbatim to a `.sql` file with no streaming worker; `E` on the Tables panel exports every relation's DDL of the database in one file, ordered by `db.DDLOrder`'s foreign-key topological sort with an alphabetical fallback on a cycle.
- [design/query-editor-and-history](design/query-editor-and-history.md) — Design Decision — `:` editor, why free-form results are materialized and paged in memory, dialect-aware statement splitting and read/write classification, unstaged DML that only confirms an unguarded DELETE/UPDATE, run cancellation, the JSON Lines history under `XDG_STATE_HOME`, and its per-connection scoping keyed by connection name with legacy entries shown everywhere.
- [design/query-editor-panel](design/query-editor-panel.md) — Design Decision — the editor as the permanent panel `[5]`, its normal/insert mode split ahead of the global keys, preview-in-column vs. editor-in-main-view, who owns focus after a run, and the fall-through to the data grid.
- [design/vim-mode-query-editor](design/vim-mode-query-editor.md) — Design Decision — a hand-rolled pure vim engine (`vim.go`) over the v2 textarea instead of vimtea; the key set (h/j/k/l, w/b/e, 0/$, gg/G, i/a/I/A/o/O, x/dd/yy/p), normal-mode enter running the statement under the caret via the span-aware splitter, pending dd/yy/gg chords with explicit reset, the wanted-column rule, the single register, and the deliberate omission of undo.
- [design/history-pane-and-placeholders](design/history-pane-and-placeholders.md) — Design Decision — the query history as a floating pane opened from the editor (`backspace`), tokenizer-based `?`/`:name` placeholder detection, and per-dialect prompt-and-bind execution that never interpolates values into SQL.
- [design/named-query-snippets](design/named-query-snippets.md) — Design Decision — named statements in a store of their own (`internal/snippets`, rewritten whole while the history appends), the name as a case-insensitive identity with a confirmed overwrite, and the Snippets section inside the existing floating pane rather than a second modal.
- [design/explain-view](design/explain-view.md) — Design Decision — `ctrl+e` runs one statement under the dialect's EXPLAIN prefix behind `Driver.Explain`, one `db.Plan` with three renderings (tree/grid/text), the plan taking over the main view instead of a modal, caret-based statement selection, and why ANALYZE is never sent.
- [design/sql-syntax-highlighting](design/sql-syntax-highlighting.md) — Design Decision — a hand-rolled, never-failing SQL scanner in `internal/sqlhl` instead of chroma; the query editor draws its own gutter, wrapping and cursor because only one of it and the textarea may wrap; colours are `[theme]` slots.
- [design/schema-aware-autocomplete](design/schema-aware-autocomplete.md) — Design Decision — the completion popup as a lipgloss layer rather than a modal, a token scan instead of a parser, schema-before-keywords ranking, a self-invalidating column cache keyed by connection+database, and quoting only where the identifier requires it.
- [design/command-log-panel](design/command-log-panel.md) — Design Decision — a single `Driver.Logger()` choke point in `internal/db` replaces per-callsite SQL echoing, merged with the UI's own status notes into one feed for the slim panel and the `@` expanded, scrollable view.
- [design/ssh-tunnels](design/ssh-tunnels.md) — Design Decision — a tunnel is a transport injected under the driver as a `DialFunc`, not a local forwarded port; host keys prompt or refuse but are never silently accepted; the tunnel's lifetime is the connection's, including a synchronous teardown on quit.
- [design/configurable-keys-and-theme](design/configurable-keys-and-theme.md) — Design Decision — `[keys]`/`[theme]` validate in `internal/ui` (not `internal/config`) and turn into a hard startup error via `ui.New() (Model, error)`; name registries for actions and colors back the "list every valid name" error messages; `Config.Clone`/`configFile` gained the deep-copy and round-trip they were missing.
- [design/read-only-connections](design/read-only-connections.md) — Design Decision — `read_only` per connection enforced in the driver session (Exec/ExecTx/writing queries), tokenizer-based write classification, UI entry points that only decorate the guard, and engine DSN flags as defence in depth.
- [design/connection-color-tags](design/connection-color-tags.md) — Design Decision — optional per-connection environment color tag rides the main view's top border and a panel `[1]`/main-view `●` marker instead of replacing the focus color, plus its use in destructive confirm modals and graceful degrade on an invalid value.
- [design/dump-and-restore](design/dump-and-restore.md) — Playbook — per-engine dump/restore command construction (`pg_dump`/`psql`, `mysqldump`/`mysql`, `mariadb-dump`/`mariadb`, SQLite `VACUUM INTO`, DuckDB `EXPORT`/`IMPORT DATABASE`), the 0600 credential-file rule that keeps passwords out of argv and the environment, process-group cancellation, the typed restore confirmation, and the local SSH forward external tools need.
- [design/ci-and-release-pipeline](design/ci-and-release-pipeline.md) — Design Decision — CI runs natively on Linux and macOS; release binaries build per-target on native runners (including a hosted arm64 Linux runner) instead of CGO cross-compiling, so DuckDB needs no build-tag gating; `goreleaser build --single-target` plus `softprops/action-gh-release` replace `goreleaser release`, which can't split a build across runners on the OSS tier.
- [design/session-restore](design/session-restore.md) — Design Decision — `session.json` under `XDG_STATE_HOME` names the last connection/database/table/tab/cursor; `Model.restoreSess` carries the target across the connect→openDatabase→openTable→page-load chain since no single closure spans all four async replies, `dialRequest.restore` plus a nilled `restoreSess` distinguishes a cancelled dial from an adopted one, and `restore_session` is a plain `bool`, opt-in (absent/false default to no restore, since issue 116).
- [design/border-embedded-panel-titles](design/border-embedded-panel-titles.md) — Design Decision — panel number, name, sub-tabs and loading marker are spliced into the box's top border by one `renderTitledBox` compositor (rebuilt from the border runes, ANSI-aware truncation) instead of taking a content row; every panel body gained that row back and each main-view renderer handed its header to `mainTitle`.
- [design/screen-mode-persistence](design/screen-mode-persistence.md) — Design Decision — `internal/config.State`/`state.toml` next to `config.toml` persists disposable UI state (starting with `screenMode`, saved on quit, stored by name) without touching the hand-edited `Config` TOML or `internal/session`'s browsing-position file.
- [design/mouse-support](design/mouse-support.md) — Design Decision — cell-motion tracking on the `tea.View`, hit-testing recomputed from the layout numbers instead of cached panel rects, second-click-is-`enter` rather than double-click, the wheel aimed by the pointer and coalesced into one scroll per frame, and the terminal wheel/selection behaviour enabling tracking costs.
- [design/clipboard-strategy](design/clipboard-strategy.md) — Design Decision — a copy tries the native clipboard, then OSC 52, then the temp-file spill; the escape sequence rides back through the update loop as `copiedMsg.osc52` because only the program may write to the tty; a bracketed paste is routed as its own message (`updatePaste`, optional `pasteHandler` on modals) so pasted text is never read as vim commands.
- [design/query-editor-ux-rework](design/query-editor-ux-rework.md) — Design Decision — issue #88's UX audit of panel [3] and the redesign: in-content mode/state status line, `H` history pane whose `enter` loads instead of runs (`r` runs), pane keys as first-class `key.Binding`s, shortened help descs so the options bar fits, and the column-packing `?` modal.
- [design/input-coalescing](design/input-coalescing.md) — Design Decision — Bubble Tea v2 builds a View per queued message, so held navigation keys backlogged; keyboard Down/Up now route through the wheel's frame coalescer (first event immediate, repeats accumulate, net delta per 16 ms flush), and an `editorCache` memoizes the query editor's tokenization, wrap geometry and rendered block so a pure cursor move styles only the visible rows.
- [design/date-time-picker](design/date-time-picker.md) — Design Decision — `db.ClassifyType` maps every dialect's DATE/TIME/TIMESTAMP spelling onto a coarse `TypeKind`, and temporal columns get a calendar+clock modal that swaps with the plain text editor through the shell's modal-replacement rule; `h`/`l` · `j`/`k` · `[`/`]` in both halves, per-field wrapping on the clock, and a mandatory raw-text escape hatch for NULL and SQL expressions.
- [design/user-documentation-site](design/user-documentation-site.md) — Design Decision — issue #146's MkDocs Material site in `userdocs/`, why the OKF wiki stays out of it, the `strict` PR-check-only workflow and local `make docs-deploy` publishing (#148, no CI write access to `gh-pages`), the `<10` Material pin, why `pymdownx.keys` is used sparingly in a case-sensitive keymap, and the test that keeps the keybinding reference in sync with `keyMap.slots()`.
- [design/main-tab-navigation-keys](design/main-tab-navigation-keys.md) — Design Decision — issue #135's `<`/`>` as the primary prev/next main-tab bindings (layout-neutral on German QWERTZ and US/UK alike), why `H`/`L` was rejected (collides with the `L` command-log alias) and why `ctrl+left`/`ctrl+right` wasn't added either, with `[`/`]` and `,`/`.` kept bound as legacy aliases.
- [design/absolute-file-paths](design/absolute-file-paths.md) — Design Decision — issue #157's `config.ResolveFilePath` expands `~` and resolves a relative SQLite/DuckDB File path to absolute inside `toConnection()` at form-submit time, why load-time resolution or a config rewrite were rejected (existing relative paths on disk are left untouched until re-saved), and why `internal/pathcomplete` needed no change.
- [design/duplicate-connection](design/duplicate-connection.md) — Design Decision — issue #156's `y` duplicate action: the existing connection form reused as a plain "new connection" submit under a unique `<name> - Copy` name, `esc` discarding outright instead of taking the create flow's engine-picker detour, and the new `secrets.Copy` (a `Rename` without the trailing delete) copying the source's keyring secret onto the new entry on save.
- [design/connection-reorder](design/connection-reorder.md) — Design Decision — issue #158's `K`/`J` move-up/move-down on panel [1], why shifted K/J beat ctrl+up/ctrl+down (terminal support, no collision with plain k/j navigation or the grid's ShiftUp/ShiftDown), `Config.MoveUp`/`MoveDown` swapping whole `Connection` structs by index, and reusing `cfg.Save()` via a small `reorderCmd` instead of a second serializer.
